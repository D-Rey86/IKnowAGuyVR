TrackedBodyAnchor.cs uses the neck-pivot/rest-rig approach in Dustin's local
EnshroudedVR project. That project's body mathematics were adapted from
https://github.com/momotori01/ENSHROUDED-VR-MOD
Source revision e670a1bdbc923c269e72b078e755f1f642ef9e65.
Original files: source/vrmod/include/evr/{body_ik,math,motion_intent}.hpp,
camera_sum_stub.asm, and the native render-pose/input-shadow integration in
motion_body_ik, motion_visual_bridge, view_resource_probe and input_pose_bridge.
The Unity implementation uses cached native skin bind poses, headset/controller
targets, reversible bone writes and an analytic leg-reach pelvis correction.
